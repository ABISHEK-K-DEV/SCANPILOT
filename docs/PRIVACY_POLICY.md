# ScanPilot — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

ScanPilot is a document scanner and PDF editor that works entirely on your device.

## What ScanPilot collects

**Nothing.** ScanPilot has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
The released Android app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

## Your documents

* Documents, page images, recognised text, signatures and settings are stored only in
  ScanPilot's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read them.
* Text recognition (OCR) and business-card reading run on your device.
* Files leave the device only when **you** choose Share, Save to device or Print, and then
  only to the app or folder you pick.
* Automatic cloud backup of the app is disabled. Uninstalling ScanPilot deletes its data.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Only while you scan a document in ScanPilot's camera. |
| Photos (system picker) | Only the images you select, to turn them into PDFs. |
| Biometrics | Optional app lock and locked folder, handled by the operating system. |

If you choose the optional **System scanner** in Settings, scanning runs in Google Play services
(Android) or Apple VisionKit (iOS) on your device, under their terms.

## Children

ScanPilot does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue in the project repository.

## Google Play "Data safety" answers

* Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
* Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? **Not applicable — no data is collected or transmitted.**
* Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? **Users delete documents in the app; uninstalling removes all data.**
